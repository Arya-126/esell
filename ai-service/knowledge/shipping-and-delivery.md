# Shipping & Delivery

## Cost
Shipping is free on ReWear — the price you see is the price you pay (converted
into your selected currency at checkout).

## Order lifecycle
Every order moves through these stages, which you can track on your dashboard:
1. **Awaiting payment** — order created, not yet paid.
2. **Paid** — payment confirmed; the seller has been notified to ship.
3. **Shipped** — the seller has dispatched the item. If they added a tracking
   number, it appears on your order.
4. **Delivered** — you confirm receipt from your dashboard, which lets you leave
   a review.

## Where is my order?
Ask the assistant "where is my order?" while signed in and it will read your
current order status, tracking number, and shipped/delivered dates. You can also
see everything under Dashboard → Orders and on the Transactions page.

## Who ships
Each seller ships their own items. If you bought several items in one cart
checkout, they may arrive separately because different sellers fulfil them.
