# Selling & Accounts

## Creating an account
Register with your name, email, and a password (at least 6 characters). New users
verify their email address before they can buy or sell. The very first account
created on a fresh install becomes the admin.

## How to sell an item
1. Click "Sell" in the navigation bar (you must be signed in and verified).
2. Add a photo, a title, an honest description, a price, a category, and the
   item's condition.
3. Publish. Your listing appears in the marketplace immediately.

You can list your price in your own currency — it is converted to USD so buyers
worldwide see it in theirs. Uploaded photos are automatically thumbnailed.

## Categories and conditions
Categories: Electronics, Clothing, Furniture, Books, Toys, Sports, Home, Other.
Conditions: New, Like New, Good, Fair, For Parts.

## Offers and negotiation
Buyers can make an offer below the asking price. Sellers can accept, decline, or
counter. Accepting an offer reserves the item at the agreed price for checkout.

## Trust, reviews and watchlists
Sellers earn a trust score from their reviews and completed sales. Buyers review
sellers after delivery. You can also watch any item to get a real-time alert if
its price drops.

## Fees
Listing items on ReWear is free.
