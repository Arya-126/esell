# Payments & Currency

## Payment methods
Checkout is powered by Stripe when live keys are configured; otherwise a built-in
demo card is used so you can try the full flow. For the demo, use card number
4242 4242 4242 4242, any future MM/YY expiry, and any 3–4 digit CVC. Only the card
brand and last four digits are ever stored — never the full number.

## Multi-currency
Product prices are stored in US Dollars and shown to you in your own currency,
converted with live exchange rates. Your currency is detected automatically and
can be changed any time from the currency picker in the top navigation bar.

When you check out, the exchange rate is locked onto your order, so the amount you
are charged never changes between checkout and payment. Your receipt shows both
the original USD amount and the amount charged in your currency.

## Carts
You can add multiple items to your cart and pay for them all with a single
payment. Each item is one of a kind, so it is reserved for you once you start
checkout.

## Receipts
Every paid order has a downloadable PDF receipt available from your dashboard and
the Transactions page.
