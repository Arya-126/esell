# Returns & Refunds

ReWear is a second-hand marketplace, so most items are sold as-is. If something
arrives not as described, you can open a refund request (a dispute) from your
dashboard.

## How to request a refund
1. Go to your Dashboard → Orders.
2. Find the order and click "Request refund".
3. Describe what went wrong. This opens a dispute and notifies both the seller
   and our support team.

Refunds can be requested on orders that are paid, shipped, or delivered. You
cannot request a refund on an order that is still awaiting payment or already
refunded.

## What happens next
The seller (or an admin) reviews the request and either approves or declines it.
If approved, the original payment is refunded — with real Stripe payments this is
a genuine refund to your card; in demo mode it is recorded instantly. You'll get a
notification either way, and the order status changes to "refunded".

## Cancellations
An order that has not been paid yet can be cancelled directly from your dashboard,
which releases the item back to the marketplace.
